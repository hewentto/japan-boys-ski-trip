Where to stay

Fourteen nights in six places. Nightly prices are per person sharing, and they are planning estimates until we pull real quotes for our exact dates and head count. February is Niseko's most expensive month, so the draft sleeps in Kutchan town, 10 to 15 minutes from the lifts, instead of at the base. Many Niseko rentals want a 5 to 7 night minimum, so hotels are the flexible fallback for a 4 night stay. Sapporo is at its yearly peak for the festival.

Getting around

Hokkaido is the only part where a car is worth considering. Tokyo and Kyoto are trains only.

What it costs

Per person, in US dollars at about ¥158 to $1 (the October 2026 level). You pay in yen over there, so these move with the exchange rate. Change the inputs to match your situation.
