Flights

We are coming from more than one city, and the flight is more fun together, so the plan is one shared flight across the Pacific out of San Francisco. Everyone gets themselves to it however works best, then we all fly on to Sapporo together. Fares below were checked on Nov 9, 2026, per person.

  • Out · Sat Feb 6All of us on United from SFO at 10:20 AM, landing Tokyo Haneda Sun Feb 7 at 2:50 PM.
  • Hop · Sun Feb 7Haneda to Sapporo together that evening, about $90 (JAL 6:35 PM, Skymark 6:40 PM).
  • Hop · Tue Feb 16Sapporo to Tokyo Haneda, Air Do or Skymark, $63–76.
  • Home · Sun Feb 21Tokyo Haneda to home. Lands the same day, work Monday.

How to search. Use something that can stitch different airlines and separate legs together, not only round trips on one airline group. Google Flights Multi-city prices one protected ticket. ITA Matrix searches almost any combination of airlines, but you book elsewhere. Kiwi.com and Skyscanner build self-transfer combinations out of separate tickets, which are often cheapest, but nobody rebooks you if the first flight runs late, so leave a long layover. We ran all three: none beat a Tokyo round trip plus a cheap flight to Sapporo.

Also know: Japan's departure tax tripled to about $19 on July 1, 2026 and is inside your ticket price.
